The bundled LearnForge WenKai Pilot font is a renamed subset covering this
pilot's text, derived on 2026-10-25. Outlines are unchanged. Font updates must
preserve this notice and the full upstream license. Other packs may require
an expanded font or an explicitly configured export font.

Pronunciation clips: locally generated synthetic Mandarin audio using the
macOS Tingting voice, at 120 words per minute, converted to mono PCM WAV
22,050 Hz. Each clip speaks the corresponding character in isolation.
Pinyin and audio transcripts are included in the character records. Connected
speech and tone changes are described in the learning notes. These are
synthetic examples and have not been reviewed by a native-speaking teacher.
